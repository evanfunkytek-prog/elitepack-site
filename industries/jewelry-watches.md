## What Jewelry Packaging Must Protect
- Rattling and movement: inserts must match product thickness exactly
- Tarnish and abrasion from low-grade linings
- Presentation: the unboxing must feel as considered as the piece itself
- Logistics: safe transit for watches, rings, necklaces and full collections
