Packaging for Electronics & Tech Accessories That Survives Transit

EliteBox Packaging is a Shenzhen manufacturer with 12+ years of experience packaging phones, earphones, smart-home devices and tech accessories for brands in 50+ countries. Anti-static EPE foam, drop-tested mailers and rigid gift boxes - from MOQ 100 pcs with free structural design.

Engineering challenges

What Electronics Packaging Has to Solve

  • Shock and drop protection for fragile, high-value devices
  • Electrostatic discharge (ESD) risk during storage and transit
  • Slim, premium branding for a category where unboxing is part of the review
  • Multi-SKU fit: cables, chargers, cases and devices in one kit
