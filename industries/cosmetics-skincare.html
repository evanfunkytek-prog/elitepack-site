Custom Cosmetics & Skincare Packaging That Sells the Product

EliteBox Packaging is a Shenzhen-based manufacturer with 12+ years of experience producing custom packaging for skincare and cosmetics brands across 50+ countries. From serum boxes with EVA cradles to magnetic rigid boxes for premium creams, we handle design, sampling and production under one roof - from MOQ 100 pcs.

Packaging problems

The Packaging Problems Cosmetics Brands Face

  • Moisture and light damage that degrades formulations before they reach the customer
  • Product movement in transit that ruins the "premium unboxing" moment
  • Regulatory paperwork for food-contact-adjacent and restricted-substance markets (EU/US)
  • Brand consistency across multiple SKUs, finishes and insert layouts

FAQs

FAQs

Q: What is the minimum order for cosmetic boxes?
A: 100 pcs per design, so you can test the market before committing to volume.

Q: Can you match my brand's Pantone color and foil?
A: Yes - CMYK and Pantone printing plus foil stamping, embossing and soft-touch lamination.

Q: Do you provide samples before bulk production?
A: Yes, physical samples ship in 5-7 days and the sample fee is refunded on your first order.

Q: What is the typical lead time?
A: 15 days for bulk (10-18 days depending on finish complexity); FOB Shenzhen or door-to-door DDP available.
