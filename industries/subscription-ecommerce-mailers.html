FAQs

FAQs

Q: Do mailers ship flat?
A: Yes - our mailers ship flat and assemble in seconds, saving warehouse space and freight cost.

Q: What is the minimum order?
A: 100 pcs per design, so you can test a new box before scaling.

Q: Can you deliver door-to-door in the US/EU?
A: Yes - we offer DDP delivery with a single landed cost, or FOB Shenzhen if you prefer your own freight.

Q: What is the lead time?
A: Bulk in 10-18 days (15 days typical); samples in 5-7 days with the fee refunded on your first order.
