## Food Packaging Challenges We Engineer Around
- Moisture and heat that ruin chocolate, baked goods and tea
- Grease and oil migration through board and print
- Headspace rules: food needs controlled allowance, never a zero-fit box
- Market compliance: FDA / EU food-contact declarations and restricted substances
