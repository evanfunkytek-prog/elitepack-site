Food-Safe Packaging Engineered for Chocolate, Tea & Confectionery

EliteBox Packaging manufactures custom food packaging in Shenzhen for chocolate brands, tea houses and food chains in 50+ countries. We combine food-safe inks and coatings with humidity-resistant structures, from rigid gift boxes to gable and mailer formats - MOQ 100 pcs, lead time from 15 days.

Engineering challenges

Food Packaging Challenges We Engineer Around

  • Moisture and heat that ruin chocolate, baked goods and tea
  • Grease and oil migration through board and print
  • Headspace rules: food needs controlled allowance, never a zero-fit box
  • Market compliance: FDA / EU food-contact declarations and restricted substances

FAQs

FAQs

Q: Are your inks and coatings food-safe?
A: Yes - we offer FDA/EU food-contact declarations, water-based inks and grease-resistant coatings.

Q: What is the MOQ?
A: 100 pcs per design, with free dieline and 3D preview.

Q: Do you allow extra headspace for baked goods?
A: Yes - we build the allowance into the dieline based on your product photos.

Q: What are your lead times?
A: Bulk 10-18 days (15 days typical); samples ship in 5-7 days with the fee refunded on your first order.
