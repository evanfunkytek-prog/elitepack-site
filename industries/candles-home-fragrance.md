## Candle Packaging Challenges
- Scent contamination: packaging must not transfer odors or be damaged by fragrance oils
- Wax movement: candles need firm cushioning against impact in transit
- Heat and humidity during storage and shipping
- Aesthetic expectations: candle buyers buy the mood, not just the wax
