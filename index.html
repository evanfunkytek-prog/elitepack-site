How it works

From Sketch to Shelf in 4 Steps

Share your idea

Send product photos, dimensions, or just a moodboard. We suggest materials and structure within 24 hours.

Free design & sample

Our engineers draft Dieline + 3D previews. Physical samples ship in 5-7 days, with the cost refunded on order.

Production

Printing, lamination, die-cutting, gluing and hand-assembly on dedicated lines with daily QC checkpoints.

QC & global shipping

Every batch passes AQL 2.5 inspection before export. We handle FOB Shenzhen or door-to-door DDP.
