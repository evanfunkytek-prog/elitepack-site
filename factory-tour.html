A Walk Through Our Factory

12,000 m² of printing presses, die-cutters and hand-assembly lines - plus the QC lab that watches over all of it.

Under one roof

Every Process, In-House

Keeping production in-house means faster samples, tighter QC and prices without middlemen. Our floor plan covers the full journey of a box:

  • Pre-press & proofing studio with color management
  • Offset, digital and flexo printing (up to 8 colors + foil)
  • Automatic lamination, spot-UV and embossing lines
  • Die-cutting, creasing and window-patching equipment
  • Rigid box, drawer box and mailer assembly lines
  • In-house QC lab - burst, drop, moisture and ink tests
Quality control

Checked at Every Step

Incoming materials

Paper grammage, board thickness and moisture verified against spec on arrival.

On-press checks

Color density read every 500 sheets; deviations stop the press immediately.

In-line assembly

Gluing, folding and magnet alignment checked by both sensors and operators.

Final AQL 2.5

Random sampling on every carton before packing. Photo report shared with you.
